SaveVault

Back up your game saves directly to your own Google Drive. No servers. No subscriptions. Just your files, your account.

2. What SaveVault Does

SaveVault is a free, open-source desktop utility that copies video game save files from your Windows PC to a folder in your own Google Drive account. It performs no other function.
