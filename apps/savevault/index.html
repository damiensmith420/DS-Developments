SaveVault

Back up your game saves directly to your own Google Drive. No servers. No subscriptions. Just your files, your account.

Your Drive, Your Data

Saves upload directly from your PC to your Google Drive account. No third-party servers ever touch your files.

🔐

Minimal Permissions

SaveVault uses the drive.file scope — it can only see files it created. Nothing else in your Drive is accessible.

Smart Conflict Resolution

If a save changed on both sides, SaveVault shows you both timestamps and lets you choose — file by file.

Single .exe file. No installer, no runtime, no dependencies. Download and run.

How It Works

  1. Sign in with your Google account via the standard OAuth consent screen.
  2. Add a game — give it a name and point SaveVault at the folder where it stores saves.
  3. Click Sync — your saves upload to a Save Games/<Game Name>/ folder in your Drive.
  4. On a new PC — sign in again, link the game to its local path, and sync back down.

1. Overview

SaveVault is a free, open-source desktop application for Windows that backs up your video game save files to your own Google Drive account. It is a personal utility — it has no backend servers, no user accounts, no analytics, and no data collection of any kind.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

SaveVault does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No telemetry or usage statistics are recorded.
  • No crash reports are sent anywhere.
  • No analytics libraries are included.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.
  • No email addresses, names, or any other personally identifiable information are collected by the application or its developer.

3. Google Account Access

To back up your saves, SaveVault asks you to sign in with your Google account using the standard OAuth 2.0 consent screen. SaveVault requests only the following permission scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This scope limits the application so that it can only see and modify files that SaveVault itself created. It cannot read, access, browse, list, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

Your OAuth token is stored locally on your own computer at:

%USERPROFILE%\.savevault\token.json

This file never leaves your machine except through the standard Google OAuth token-refresh mechanism that communicates directly with Google's servers. The developer of SaveVault never receives, sees, or has access to your token, your email address, or any content of your Google Drive.

4. Your Save Files

Your save files are uploaded directly from your PC to your own Google Drive account using the Google Drive API. They are not routed through any third-party server. The developer of SaveVault has no access to your files.

5. Third-Party Services

SaveVault interacts only with Google's OAuth 2.0 and Google Drive v3 APIs. Your use of Google services is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the new version will be committed to the SaveVault GitHub repository with an updated date. The current version always reflects the current release.

7. Contact

SaveVault is an open-source project. For questions or concerns, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
