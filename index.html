Philosophy

Why independent?

Big software often comes with big strings attached — telemetry, paywalls, accounts you didn't ask for, and features that get removed in the next update. As an independent developer I answer to my users, not investors. Every decision is made based on what's actually useful, what's actually fair, and what's actually worth shipping. That's the deal I'm making with anyone who uses my work.
